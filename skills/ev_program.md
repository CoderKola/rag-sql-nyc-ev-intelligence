# PlugNYC — Official Program Facts

## What PlugNYC is
NYC DOT's public EV charging network at municipal parking garages. Includes Level 2 chargers (up to 240V, ~7–11 kW) and DC fast chargers (480V+, 50–150 kW). Free to use the parking lot infrastructure; drivers pay per kWh for electricity.

## Rates
- DC fast chargers: $0.39 per kWh
- DC fast charger users get a $9 credit toward their first hour of parking
- Level 2 rates: check posted signage at each facility (not available in this dataset)

## How to pay
EV Connect App (iOS/Android) or credit card at the charger. No account required for credit card. EV Connect account recommended for tracking sessions.
